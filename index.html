The menu

Signature grooming

From classic scissor work to razor-clean finishes — each service is performed with precision tools, premium products, and an eye for balance and proportion.

Precision haircut

Consultation, shampoo refresh, tailored cut, and styled finish — scissor or clipper work shaped to your hair type and lifestyle.

Beard sculpt & shape

Outline, taper, and volume control with hot steam and straight-razor detailing for a clean silhouette that holds its line.

Hot towel shave

Traditional prep, warm towels, rich lather, and single-blade passes for the smoothest skin and sharpest edge.

Skin fades & line-ups

Crisp tapers, drop fades, and edge-ups with careful blending — built for sharp transitions and a polished, camera-ready look.

Hair & scalp treatment

Deep cleanse, conditioning mask, and scalp massage to restore shine, softness, and healthy growth between cuts.

Junior cuts

Patient chair time for young clients — neat school styles, first fades, and a calm environment parents trust.

Our house

A slower rhythm. A higher standard.

Vanguard is an independent barbershop built on technique, consistency, and hospitality. We keep the room intentional: low lighting, curated music, and stations spaced so every guest has room to breathe.

Our barbers train continuously on modern fades, classic tailoring, and skin-safe razor work — so whether you are here for a quick tidy or a full reset, you leave looking deliberate.
